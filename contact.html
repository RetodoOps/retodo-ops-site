Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

⏱️

How quickly can you deliver?

Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours for documents up to 2,000 words. Larger projects are scoped individually. Rush delivery is available — contact us to discuss your deadline.

💰

How is pricing calculated?

We price per source word, based on language pair, subject matter, and service level (translation only vs. full TEP). Request a quote to get an exact figure for your project.

🔒

How do you protect confidential documents?

All files are transferred via encrypted channels. We sign NDAs on request, and our systems are GDPR compliant. Translators work under strict confidentiality agreements.

📋

What formats do you accept?

We accept Word, PDF, PowerPoint, Excel, HTML, XML, XLIFF, and most other common formats. We return translations in the same format as received where possible.

✅

What is your quality process?

All translations go through our TEP process: Translation by a native-speaker specialist, Editing by a second linguist, and Proofreading for final quality check. Certified to ISO 17100:2015.

🔄

Can you handle ongoing projects?

Yes — we offer retainer agreements and ongoing partnerships for clients with regular translation needs. Long-term relationships also enable us to build consistent terminology and brand voice.
