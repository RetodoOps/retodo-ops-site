How we work

Your workflow stays in control

We adapt to the account rather than forcing the account into a generic process.

01

Brief

We confirm language, scope, subject matter, files, deadline, tools and review level.

02

Match

We assign an approved native-target linguist with the relevant experience and availability.

03

Produce

The linguist follows your TM, glossary, style guide, instructions and platform requirements.

04

Check & Deliver

We complete the agreed linguistic and technical QA, then deliver through your chosen channel.
