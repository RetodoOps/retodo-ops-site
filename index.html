Who I work with

I work with small and growing companies that need reliable operational support, but don’t need — or don’t want — a full-time hire.

Typical clients include:

  • Founders and small teams overloaded with admin, payments, and coordination work
  • Companies that want one dependable operational point of contact
  • EU companies operating in or entering the Bulgarian market

What I help with

General Operations & Administration

  • End-to-end operational and administrative support
  • Ownership of HR, compliance, and contract-related documentation workflows, including preparation, signing, coordination, and archiving
  • Coordination with external partners (accountants, lawyers, insurers, occupational medicine etc.) and vendors as part of day-to-day operations
  • Acting as a local point of contact for teams operating in Bulgaria where physical presence or locally signed documents are required

Finance Operations

  • Billing and invoice creation across complex pricing models and various systems
  • Verification and reconciliation of detailed charges against client systems and agreements
  • Purchase order coordination

  • Bookkeeping support for internal processes, in coordination with external accountants

Process Improvement & Operational Clarity

  • Identifying operational inefficiencies and areas for improvement
  • Proposing and implementing practical process improvements where they add real value
  • Improving visibility and clarity around financial and operational reporting

Recruitment & People Support (add-on)

  • Job postings and employer branding support
  • CV screening and interview coordination
  • Interview participation
  • Onboarding documentation and process support

How we work

I work as an extension of your team, taking ownership of defined operational areas rather than handling isolated tasks.

We agree on scope, priorities, and communication upfront. I manage all work in my own internal system to ensure clarity, continuity, and follow-through.

You are not required to use any specific tools. Requests can be shared via email or discussed directly, and I translate them into structured, trackable work internally.

Access levels, responsibilities, confidentiality, and availability expectations are clearly defined in a service agreement.

Pricing

I work on a monthly retainer, based on the defined scope, volume of work, and level of involvement.

This model is typically more cost-efficient and flexible than hiring an in-house operations or admin role, while providing continuity and accountability.

Pricing is discussed individually after an initial conversation.

About me

I’m comfortable taking ownership, handling sensitive information, and coordinating across multiple stakeholders, internal teams, external partners, and clients.

Where it adds real value, I proactively bring structure to inefficient or unclear processes and build new ones when operational needs require it.

My focus is on protecting the company’s interests while bringing clarity, reliability, and calm execution — without unnecessary complexity.
