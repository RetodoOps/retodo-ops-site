Our Process

How We Work

A rigorous four-stage workflow ensures every translation meets our ISO 17100 quality standard — from first brief to final delivery.

1

Brief & Scope

You share your files and project requirements. We confirm cost and timeline within 2 hours — leveraging TM matches to give you the best price instantly.

2

AI + TM Draft

Our AI engines and TM database generate a high-quality draft. Existing segments are matched automatically, slashing turnaround time and cost for repeat content.

3

Human Post-Editing

A senior native-speaking editor refines the draft — fixing nuance, tone, and cultural fit. The result reads as if written by a native, not a machine.

4

QA & Delivery

An independent QA reviewer signs off before delivery. You receive the finished file in your original format, or directly to your repo via GitHub Actions.

name: Localization

on:
  push:
    paths:
      - 'src/i18n/en/**'

jobs:
  translate:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
      - uses: retodo-ops/translate@v2
        with:
          source: en
          targets: [no, sv, da, fi]
          api-key: ${{ secrets.RETODO_KEY }}
