Apply as a Translator

We welcome applications from qualified native Nordic-language specialists with proven subject-matter expertise and professional CAT-tool experience.

What We Look For

Retodo Ops works with native-target translators who combine linguistic skill with verifiable subject-matter expertise. Applications are reviewed against current language-pair and account needs.

🌍

Languages

English, German and French into Norwegian (Bokmål or Nynorsk), Swedish, Danish, Finnish and Icelandic

🎓

Qualifications

Translation degree or equivalent subject-matter expertise. Native speaker of the target language. Professional experience preferred.

💻

CAT Tools

Proficiency with at least one industry-standard CAT tool: SDL Trados Studio, memoQ, Phrase, or Crowdin

✅

What Happens Next

Shortlisted applicants may be asked for references, credentials and a qualification test. Approval does not guarantee immediate assignments.
