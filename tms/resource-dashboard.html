Freelancer Framework Agreement

For independent linguists and language-service companies

Retodo and the Service Provider are each a “Party” and together the “Parties”.

1. Independent relationship

The Service Provider acts as an independent contractor, not as an employee, agent, partner or exclusive supplier of Retodo. The Service Provider controls how services are performed, subject to project specifications, deadlines, security requirements and quality standards, and is responsible for taxes, social-security obligations, permits, equipment and insurance applicable to its business.

2. Project assignments

Each assignment requires a written purchase order or equivalent written confirmation (“Project PO”) stating scope, languages, service, files, deadline and compensation. Retodo issues the Project PO only after the Service Provider has confirmed availability, scope, rate and deadline. Once issued, the Project PO constitutes the final written assignment and no further acceptance is required. The Project PO and this Agreement form the complete terms for that assignment.

3. Performance standards

The Service Provider shall deliver accurate, complete and fit-for-purpose work; follow instructions, terminology, style guides and CAT-tool requirements; perform appropriate self-review and QA; immediately flag ambiguity, corrupted files, conflicts, capacity issues or likely delay; and not introduce machine translation or generative-AI tools unless Retodo expressly authorizes them in writing for the project.

4. Personal performance and subcontracting

The Service Provider may not subcontract, share files, or allow another person to access project materials without Retodo’s prior written approval. Approved subcontractors must be bound by obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. The Service Provider remains fully responsible for their work.

5. Confidentiality and security

The Service Provider shall keep Client and Retodo information confidential, use it only for the assignment, apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, and promptly report any loss, unauthorized access, disclosure or suspected security incident. Project materials may not be uploaded to public, consumer or unapproved cloud, AI or MT services.

6. Data protection

Where project materials contain personal data, the Service Provider shall process it only on documented instructions from Retodo, limit access, maintain confidentiality and security, assist with incidents and data-subject requests, and delete or return it when instructed. If legally required, the Parties shall execute a separate data-processing addendum before access.

7. Fees and invoicing

Fees are set in each Project PO. Retodo operates two freelancer invoicing cycles each month: the 15th and the last working day of the month. Completed Project POs are approved for invoicing on the next applicable cycle date after Retodo accepts the delivery. Work approved by the 15th may be invoiced on the 15th; work approved after that cutoff may be invoiced on the last working day. The Service Provider shall submit one legally valid invoice for the POs approved in that cycle. Retodo shall pay undisputed amounts within 60 calendar days calculated from the applicable invoicing date, by bank transfer in the agreed currency. The Service Provider bears receiving-bank charges and is responsible for its taxes. Retodo may withhold only amounts genuinely disputed and shall explain the dispute promptly.

8. Corrections and quality issues

The Service Provider shall correct substantiated errors attributable to its work promptly and without additional charge. If urgent correction is necessary and the Service Provider is unavailable or fails to correct within the required time, Retodo may arrange correction and seek reasonable direct costs to the extent caused by the breach. Repeated material quality or deadline failures may result in suspension or termination.

Where a substantiated quality failure, missed deadline, incomplete delivery or other material non-conformity reduces the value or usability of the Services, Retodo may, acting reasonably and proportionately, apply a reduction to the affected Project PO, set off documented direct correction costs, or agree another commercial adjustment. Retodo shall notify the Service Provider in writing of the grounds and calculation. Any reduction shall relate only to the affected work and shall not exceed the unpaid amount under the relevant Project PO, except for separately recoverable direct losses permitted under this Agreement.

9. Intellectual property

To the fullest extent permitted by law, all transferable economic rights in project deliverables are assigned to Retodo upon payment of the relevant invoice, for onward transfer or licensing to the Client. The Service Provider waives or agrees not to assert moral rights to the extent legally possible. Pre-existing tools and know-how remain with their owner, but the Service Provider grants rights necessary to use the deliverable.

10. Records, retention and deletion

The Service Provider shall retain only records required by law or reasonably necessary for invoicing and defense of claims. On request or project completion, it shall return or securely delete project files and confirm deletion, except for legally required archival copies, which remain protected.

11. Non-solicitation and non-circumvention

During an assignment and for 12 months after it, the Service Provider shall not knowingly bypass Retodo to solicit or contract directly for substantially the same services with a Client first introduced through Retodo, unless Retodo gives written consent. This does not restrict a demonstrable pre-existing relationship or general marketing not targeted at that Client.

12. Warranties and liability

The Service Provider warrants that work is original or properly authorized, does not knowingly infringe third-party rights, and complies with applicable law. Each Party is responsible for direct loss caused by its breach. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, except where such limitation is prohibited. Nothing limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, confidentiality breach, unlawful data processing, or infringement caused by the responsible Party.

13. Term and termination

This Agreement starts on the effective date and continues until terminated by either Party on 30 days’ written notice. Retodo may suspend assignments or terminate immediately for serious confidentiality/security breach, fraud, unauthorized subcontracting or AI/MT use, repeated material quality failure, or other irremediable breach. Termination does not affect issued assignments, accrued payment rights, confidentiality, IP, data, non-circumvention or liability provisions.

14. Governing law and disputes

Bulgarian law governs this Agreement. The Parties shall first attempt good-faith resolution; unresolved disputes fall within the jurisdiction of the competent Bulgarian courts, unless a Project PO or signed amendment expressly provides otherwise.

15. General

This Agreement and issued Project POs constitute the entire agreement. A Project PO overrides this Agreement only where it expressly identifies the clause being varied. Amendments must be in writing. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted. Notices are sent to the stated email addresses. Invalid provisions are severed and the remainder continues.

Electronic execution through the TMS. Retodo signs this Agreement electronically when an authorized internal user unlocks the Compliance phase for the Service Provider. The Service Provider signs this Agreement electronically by selecting Accept and sign while authenticated in Retodo's TMS. Each Party expressly agrees, pursuant to Article 13(4) of the Bulgarian Electronic Document and Electronic Trust Services Act, that the electronic signature created through its respective TMS action has legal effect equivalent to that Party's handwritten signature. The system shall bind both actions to the exact immutable Agreement version and record the signatory identity, account, email, UTC date and time, Agreement version, document SHA-256 hash and audit trail. This Agreement becomes effective when the Service Provider applies the second and final signature.
