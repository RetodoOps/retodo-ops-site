Who We Are

A Specialist LSP for the Nordic World

We built Retodo Ops on a single belief: that precise language is the foundation of successful international business.

Our Story

Retodo Ops was founded by language professionals who saw a gap in the market: businesses entering Nordic markets needed more than generic translation — they needed a partner who truly understood Scandinavian cultures, industries, and regulatory environments.

We started with a focused team of native Norwegian, Swedish, and Danish translators and rapidly expanded to cover all five major Nordic languages. Today, we support clients ranging from European SMEs to global enterprises across every major industry.

Our approach has always been the same: pair every project with a native-speaking translator who has real expertise in the subject matter, then run it through our rigorous TEP quality process before it reaches your desk.

What Drives Us

Our Values

Everything we do flows from three core commitments that define how we work with our clients and our translators.

🎯

Precision

We treat every word as important. Our multi-stage TEP process, specialist translators, and client-specific glossaries exist to eliminate errors and ensure your message lands exactly as intended.

🤝

Partnership

We work as an extension of your team, not a transactional vendor. Long-term relationships mean better consistency, faster turnaround, and a partner who understands your voice.

🌐

Cultural Intelligence

Translation without cultural awareness produces content that feels foreign. Our native translators ensure your communication resonates authentically in each Nordic market.
